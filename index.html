01 / Featured research · Biomedical machine learning

A practical approach to triple-negative breast cancer subtyping

Can molecular TNBC subtypes be distinguished without full transcriptome profiling? Compared clinical variables with a focused six-marker biomarker panel in 175 TCGA cases, using repeated cross-validation, per-class evaluation, and permutation testing.

0.60 ± 0.08Macro F1 · 25 test folds
61.8%Cross-validated accuracy
p ≤ 0.01Label-permutation test

What changed: Routine clinical features alone produced macro F1 near 0.25, while the six-marker random forest reached 0.60. Validation used 5 repetitions of stratified 5-fold cross-validation. The 1,000-gene transcriptomic reference reached 0.80 macro F1.

Research limitation: The marker panel was represented by corresponding mRNA expression, not measured IHC stain scores. Testing with actual IHC measurements and an external patient cohort remains necessary before clinical use.
